## Payment

- Cash on delivery only. No card/UPI/wallet checkout; payment details are never collected online.

## Ordering & daily cutoff

- Everything is made fresh in small batches. Order by 11:59 PM IST for next-morning delivery.
- At 12:00 AM IST a new daily batch starts, so orders placed then are scheduled for the following morning.
- Dates use India Standard Time.

## Delivery area

- Only the Bachupally locality of Hyderabad and nearby streets for now.
- Orders outside the active delivery area may be declined.

## Login

- WhatsApp phone-number login is planned (currently a preview on the website).
- First-time customers give only a name and delivery location; no email or password.

## Goodies instead of coupons

- No percentage discounts or coupon codes.
- First orders include a free surprise goodie (e.g. a complimentary ragi malt with a sprout bowl), and referral of a friend earns both of you a goodie.
- Goodies appear in the order summary once confirmed, not at checkout.

## Cancellations & refunds

- Cancellation can be requested before 11:59 PM IST on the order date if preparation has not started; after the daily batch closes it may not be possible.
- Delivered food cannot be returned for safety reasons; problems are reviewed if reported on the delivery day.
- No online refunds (nothing is paid online).

## Storage guidance

- Ragi drinks are best warm and fresh.
- Sprouts and salads should be refrigerated and eaten within a day.
- Fresh juices should be kept chilled and finished the same day.
